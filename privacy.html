DrillMaster Pro

Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026 (added optional research data)

In short: DrillMaster Pro keeps your well data on your own device. If you opt in, the app shares anonymous usage (which screens and tools are used) and, if you choose, your job role, country and feedback, so the developer can decide what to build. Nothing is sold, there are no adverts, and you can switch sharing off or delete it at any time.

Who we are

DrillMaster Pro (“the app”) is developed by Dixitkumar Suthar, India. Contact: Dxsuthar009@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter (wells, kill and trip sheets, reports, tasks, calculations, settings, photos, videos and voice notes) is saved only in the app’s storage on your phone, tablet or computer. It is not sent to the developer or any server operated by the developer. Uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Erase or Security and privacy → Erase a well, deletes it.

Device permissions

  • Camera: only when you take a site photo, record a video or scan a QR label.
  • Microphone: only when you record a video or dictate a voice note. On some devices dictation is processed by the operating system’s speech service (Google or Apple) under their own privacy terms.
  • Location: only when you tap “Well location” to save a well’s GPS position. It is stored on your device with that well.
  • Photos and files: only when you choose a file to import or save an export.

You can refuse or withdraw any permission in your device settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

Optional research data (opt-in)

When the app first opens it asks whether you want to help improve it. Only if you say yes, it sends:

  • Anonymous usage: a random ID created on your device (not linked to your name), which screens, formulas and exports you use, session length, app errors, app version, platform (website, Android or iOS app), device type (phone, tablet or computer), operating system, language, unit system and time zone.
  • Profile, only if you fill it in: job role, company type, onshore or offshore, years of experience and country. Your email address only if you tick that you agree to be contacted.
  • Feedback you send: rating, comments, most-used module and feature votes.

It never sends well names, depths, pressures or any value you type, nor photos, notes, reports or GPS position.

Purpose: deciding which features, units and languages to build, finding errors, and (if you agreed) contacting you about research. Legal basis: your consent. Where: a Google Sheet controlled by the developer (Google LLC processes it under Google Workspace terms). Kept: up to 24 months, then deleted or fully aggregated. Never sold or shared with advertisers.

Your choices: turn sharing off in Settings → Privacy and research; see exactly what is sent with See what is sent; remove everything linked to your anonymous ID with Delete my research data; or email the developer with your anonymous ID. These rights apply under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and the UK/EU GDPR.

Data you choose to send

Some features send data only when you start them: sharing a report or well file (through your device’s share sheet to the app you pick), encrypted backups you save, and webhooks you configure yourself to your own address. The developer never receives a copy.

Internet use

The app works offline. When online it may download fonts and document-export libraries from public content networks (Google Fonts, jsDelivr, cdnjs). These services can see your device’s IP address as part of normal web requests.

Children

The app is a professional engineering tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Security

Optional app lock with PIN and fingerprint or face unlock, and AES-256 encrypted backups, are available in Security and privacy. Keep your device and backups protected.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
